HOW TO USE (DEMO):
  1. This demo uses your mobile phone camera as the input source.
  2. Mount phone at baseline or net post, stable angle.
  3. Tap START to enable camera.
  4. Tap CALIBRATE, then drag the 4 yellow dots to the court corners visible on screen.
  5. Detection uses ball color (yellow-green). Bright daylight works best.
  6. Calls appear on the top-down court map. Send them to scoring with the buttons above.
DISCLAIMER: This is a single-camera approximation, NOT real Hawk-Eye. Professional systems use 10+ synchronized high-speed cameras. Calls here are best-effort and not officiation-grade. Lighting, camera angle, and ball visibility heavily affect accuracy.

◆ ABOUT

MATA SA AGILA v1.1
Cebuano: "Eye of the Eagle"
Powered by CYRABELL

Single-camera tennis ball tracking, scoring engine, shot classification & heatmap analytics.

INPUT (DEMO): Mobile phone camera. Production roadmap includes dedicated multi-camera court rig.

METHOD: Real-time HSV color segmentation isolates the ball; trajectory smoothing identifies bounces; 4-point homography maps screen coords to top-down court. Shot type is classified from approach angle, arc shape & post-bounce velocity. Heatmap uses Gaussian-blur accumulation over court space.

BACKEND: Google Apps Script + Google Sheets (GET-based, zero auth).

BUILT FOR: Practice sessions, club matches, training analysis. NOT a substitute for ITF-certified line-calling systems.
